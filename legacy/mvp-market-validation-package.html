What is this package?

Are you considering building a digital product?

We help you validate concepts quickly and cost-effectively through a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

By validating before full-scale development, we ensure scalability based on real data - not assumptions - saving you from burning capital.

Delivery

Included in the MVP and validation package

Discovery

Mapping target audience needs, pain points, and market opportunities.

‍Goal Setting: defining the project objectives.
‍Product audit: exploring current products and operations.
‍Research planning: compiling a research strategy.
‍Research execution: conducting market research, competitor analysis, and user research.
‍Value proposition: designing the value proposition.
‍Presentation: presenting research findings.

Design

Designing the MVP features and the business model.

‍Prioritization: feature prioritization and MVP scoping.
‍User stories: creating user stories based on the features.
‍Prototyping: designing and testing the initial prototype.
‍Business modeling: designing and validating the business model.
‍Communication strategy: compiling communication guides.
‍Approval: iterating designs and getting approval.

Development

Developing key features and the business model.

‍Software Development: building the MVP software.
‍Quality Assurance: testing, delivery, and deployment.

Delivery

NOT Included in the MVP and validation package

Scope Changes

While the MVP focuses on core features to provide market validation, the full concept can be developed in a later phase or separate agreement. The fixed project price applies only to the scope defined in discovery; any additional work is billed on a time & materials basis.

Full-scale development

• Development is based on Sonar's own recommended technology stack to ensure speed and security.
• Solid infrastructure for high-level scaling is typically part of a later phase, not the MVP.

Support

While operation is not included in the MVP package, we offer ongoing maintenance and operational support through a separate monthly time & material model.

Data Collection

Implementing analytics tools is included only to the extent required for the specific validation and the MVP.

Limitations

• Based on our assessment, we may recommend a discovery-only phase or an alternative solution to ensure your product becomes its best version possible.
• To ensure high quality and guarantee our 12-week delivery, we work in close partnership with you, requiring your active engagement and resource commitment throughout the process.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

What techologies will you use?

expand_more

Following discovery, we propose the best-fit technology to ensure quick market entry. We prioritize features addressing key user pain points, aligning the stack with your specific MVP requirements.

Who is this for?

expand_more

Our MVP package serves SMEs, startups, and larger enterprises seeking quick market entry or real-world validation. We partner with domain experts who value structured validation over assumptions.

Are AI features included?

expand_more

Yes, if AI agents are part of the defined MVP scope, we can fully integrate them to solve core user pain points.

What if I need more than the MVP package?

expand_more

If our assessment reveals that the MVP package isn't your best path, we will propose a tailored solution better suited to your goals. Any new requirements emerging during the project are managed via a mutually agreed-upon time and material model.

Do you provide support?

expand_more

While operation and support are not included in the MVP package, we offer these services through a separate fixed-price monthly agreement.

How much does it cost?

expand_more

During the proposal phase, we provide a fixed estimate for the defined MVP scope. Any additional requirements are billed via a mutually agreed-upon time and material model.
