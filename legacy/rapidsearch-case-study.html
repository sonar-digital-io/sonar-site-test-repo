UX & market discovery for a Shopify app

Learn how we uncovered that an "app lottery" market was the true cause of a 53% uninstall rate for the 4.9-star-rated app.

About the client

Rapidsearch is a bootstrapped Shopify app with a 4.9-star rating and "Built for Shopify" badge, founded in 2019.

It provides AI-powered search and filtering for 5,000+ merchants and handles 33 million daily queries.

Despite its technical excellence and 400+ five-star reviews, the small team operates in a "hyper-saturated" market, making scaling challenging.

The "same-day" leak

Rapidsearch faced a critical retention gap: 53% of users uninstalled the app on the same day they installed it. Often within minutes.

This "same-day uninstall" rate led to wasted acquisition costs and lost future revenue.

The team didn't know whether the uninstalls were caused by bugs, poor onboarding, or just the way the market works.

Strategic alignment

The project provided the evidence needed to stop chasing the unfixable same-day uninstall rate.

We uncovered that Rapidsearch operates in an "app lottery" reality, where users randomly choose competing apps and uninstall them after a bad first impression.

While technical sync improvements reduced uninstalls from 53% to 38%, our research delivered a "stop" signal for over-investing in impossible retention goals.
