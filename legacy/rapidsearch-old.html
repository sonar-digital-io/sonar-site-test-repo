Reducing Rapidsearch's uninstall rate

We reduced the Rapidsearch app's uninstall rate from 50% to 32% in 3 months with UX research and onboarding optimization.

/ The problem

Rapidsearch is a Shopify search plugin competing in an "app lottery" market where a bad first impression means uninstall:

  • 50% of users deleted the Rapidsearch app instantly
  • Every uninstall wastes acquisition cost and future revenue
  • and limited UX insight prevented clear roadmap decisions

Rapidsearch's app had a 4.9 rating on Shopify. Strong product.

But they had limited insight into high uninstall rates and why many users never became paying customers. This left growth opportunities untapped.

"Despite a great product and 4.9 rating on Shopify, we lacked visibility into why users were uninstalling, resulting in lost revenue."

/ The process

Business objectives & success criteria alignment
Data collection: user research, interviews, reviews & bug reports
Full UX audit &
data analysis
20 action items & improvement recommendations
Clear development priorities integrated into a roadmap
"Sonar made blind spots visible and put them on a roadmap. They had an immediate impact."
"We were looking for incremental progress and got more than we expected."

/ The Results

Uninstall rate reduced from 50% to 32%.
User acquisition cost reduced by approximately one-third.
20 data-driven roadmap items grounded in user and market insights.

Before launch, we focused on validating how people interacted with the new visual editor. Instead of testing basic whiteboard usability, we looked at whether users could confidently design and connect data flows. We set Illustrator, Shaderforge, Blender benchmarks. The in-house data team became early adopters, using the first prototype and giving feedback that directly shaped the roadmap. What started as a test quickly turned into a co-creation process with the team itself.

Once the prototype was live, the challenge was no longer just technical. Success depended on convincing client-side professionals to adopt the tool and champion it inside their organizations. By highlighting clear benefits, such as faster integrations, easier control, and visible results, we turned skeptics into advocates. Even those who had preferred command-line scripts became some of the strongest supporters of the visual editor.
